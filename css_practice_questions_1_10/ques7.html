Typography & Text Styling

Why Good Typography Matters

Typography controls how comfortably people read and understand content. A strong visual hierarchy helps readers find important ideas quickly.

Modern websites often combine different font families, weights and sizes to create a clear and balanced reading experience.

Good spacing improves readability. Highlighted text can draw attention, while emphasized text can add meaning without overwhelming the reader.
